Escapes#

Backslash + syntax = literal. Backslash + other = stays.

Inline style#

**not bold**, __not italic__, ==not underline==, ~~not strike~~.

`not code`, [not link], ![not image], |not pipe.

/'not comment'/.

Links and images#

Escaped \| and \] stay inside.

a|b label with ] bracket

Block prefixes#

Backslash stops blocks.

> not quote> still paragraph

#1 not heading

* not list+ not list- not list^ not list= not list

--- not rule

<! not codeblock

1. not ordered list

| not gutter

Backslash itself#

\ = one backslash.

C:\path stays. \a stays. Trailing \
